{format_rules}
- Annotate only the input sentence. The guidance JSON string supplies the sentence's language tag and the surrounding source paragraph as context; context may resolve who or what a mention refers to, but never copy a span from the context and never translate, normalize, or repair the input.
- Emit only spans that carry one of the allowed tags. Never emit an element whose type is O, none, other, or any name outside the allowed list: a surface that is not PII is simply omitted from the array.
- Every emitted surface must be one contiguous substring copied character-for-character, including whitespace and punctuation inside the span. Return annotations in increasing start-position order, with the longer span first when starts are equal, one element per distinct occurrence and tag. A reference may properly contain independently meaningful non-reference spans, with different intervals; preserve both. Do not emit crossing spans, overlapping references, or overlapping non-reference spans.
- The allowed-tag definitions are the annotation scope. Label every occurrence that satisfies one, including repeated mentions and public or historical people, organizations, facilities, places, monetary amounts, and demographic attributes; do not apply a separate legal or privacy threshold.
- Use the mention's function in context. A person-looking string used as a company, venue, product, hospital, university, fictional or media title, or other non-person entity is not a person name.
- person_name is one maximal span: every component of the name together with an attached honorific such as Mr., Dr., or Frau. Never split one person's name into component spans. An explicitly written profession, office, nationality, or other descriptor beside the name is a separate demographic_attribute.
- person_reference is a pronoun, possessive, relative pronoun, nominal, or role phrase that the sentence or supplied context grounds to a specific person or definite group of people: "she", "who", "the applicant", "his mother", "the patient", "their lawyers", "our legal team's director". Include the full referential phrase with its article, determiner, or possessive; do not also emit a contained pronoun or determiner as a second reference. A proper name, pseudonym, recognizable alias, or anonymized initial functioning as a name is person_name, never person_reference. Generic, hypothetical, universally quantified, or attributive mentions remain untagged.
- organization_reference is a pronoun, possessive, anaphoric, or nominal mention grounded to a specific organization or definite institutional group but that is not its name: "it" referring to a named company, "the company", "the domestic courts", "the ministry", "our legal team". Include the full referential phrase with its article or determiner; do not also emit a contained pronoun or determiner as a second reference. A proper institutional name, acronym, alias, conventional official short name, or explicitly declared short name is organization, never organization_reference. A common institutional noun used attributively or in a generic proposition remains untagged.
- These reference rules apply to equivalents in the input language, including standalone pronouns and possessives. Tag a pronoun only when its referent is a specific person, definite group of people, organization, or definite institutional group. Dummy/expletive pronouns ("it" in "it is raining"), references to objects or propositions, and unresolved or generic pronouns remain untagged. Context can ground a referent without giving its name; do not invent a span for an omitted subject.
- A nationality adjective is not automatically protected_attribute. When it is an attribute of a specific person and no more specific tag applies, use demographic_attribute. Religion, ethnicity, political opinion, trade-union membership, and sexual orientation of a specific person are protected_attribute.
- government_id is limited to a personal identifier issued by a government or state register. A customer, patient, employee, policy, case, order, or other organization-issued number is record_identifier; a bank account or wallet is bank_account_number; a card number is payment_card_data.
- Identifier, contact, account and credential tags require the actual value to be written in the input. A field name, category word, document type, form label, or instruction to enter a value is untagged even when it refers to a specific person's data. Thus "enter your password", "forgot my username", "new PIN", "account number" and "UDID" have no identifier or credential span by themselves. In "password: sunset42", label only "sunset42" as credential. Explicitly masked value characters in a value position still count as a value; do not confuse them with the field's name.
- When a written value is explicitly used as a password, access code, secret key or authentication token, label that value credential even if its characters look like a URL, email address, username or ordinary name. The stated use takes precedence over the string's appearance; this does not make the surrounding field name a credential.
- A device product/model name and a software version are not device_identifier or ip_address. Use their stated function in context even if a version looks like an IP address. A written hostname that identifies a particular device can be device_identifier.
- A literal account handle, forum username, or poster identity is username rather than person_name. Separately label a person's written name when the text supplies it.
- A monetary amount includes its currency unit inside one span. A non-currency count, percentage, or measure that is quasi-identifying in context is quantity; a generic elapsed duration is untagged.
- Generic, class-level, and predicative nominals are untagged. An indefinite but specific introduction can be a reference. When a reference phrase contains an independently meaningful organization name, person name, age, or other non-reference entity, emit the full reference and the contained entity with their exact original boundaries. For example, "Google's lawyer" contains a person_reference over the whole phrase and organization over "Google". Do not shorten the reference to avoid nesting; flattening is a separate downstream operation.
- An organization name excludes a leading article or generic head noun unless that text is part of the proper name. A named facility containing a place name is one organization or location span; do not also label the place inside it.
- Relative or deictic time ("the same day", "last spring") and years embedded in statute, standard, reporter, or series names are untagged. A calendar date with an attached clock time is one date span.
- An amount whose currency unit is missing or corrupted is not monetary_amount. A product model number, standard number, or company discount code is untagged unless it identifies a person's record or is a secret.
- A health fact or personal attribute is labeled only when stated about a specific person or definite group, not in generic advice, product copy, or job requirements.
- Span boundaries exclude surrounding punctuation, prepositions, and case-marking function words that are separate tokens.
- Prefer the most specific applicable tag; if unsure between two tags, use the one whose definition fits the mention's function in context.
- Before output, scan once for names and grounded person references, including pronouns; attributes; organizations and grounded organization references, including pronouns; locations, administrative areas, and addresses; dates and times; identifiers, contact, financial, credential, and digital spans; health facts; and contextual quantities. Do not invent an entity or label a bare number without matching context.
